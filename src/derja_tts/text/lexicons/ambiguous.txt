# Words that exist both in Arabizi and in English/French.
# The normalizer decides from the neighbours: if the words around it are
# mostly Derja (Arabic script or Arabizi) it reads them as Arabizi, if they
# are mostly Latin it keeps them Latin. A tie goes to Derja.
#   la  : لا (no)        vs French "la"
#   ma  : ما             vs French "ma"
#   men : من             vs English "men"
#   min : من             vs English "min"
#   win : وين (where)    vs English "win"
# Particles like w, l, f, bel, ki are NOT here on purpose: in a Derja script,
# "epochs bel Adam optimizer" still means "epochs with the Adam optimizer".
la	لا
ma	ما
men	من
min	من
win	وين
